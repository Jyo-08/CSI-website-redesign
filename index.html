WHO ARE WE?

CSI VIT Chennai is a student-led technical chapter that brings together passionate learners, developers, designers and innovators. We organize workshops, hackathons, coding challenges and tech events that help students explore technology, build real-world skills and grow as a community.

MISSION

To empower students with technical knowledge, collaboration opportunities and hands-on learning experiences.

VISION

To build a strong student community that inspires innovation, leadership and impactful problem-solving.

COMMUNITY

A space where students connect, learn, share ideas and work together on technology-driven initiatives.
